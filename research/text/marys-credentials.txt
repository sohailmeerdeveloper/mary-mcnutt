Transforming Lives Through Coaching and Empowerment.

Mary McNutt’s areas of expertise include transforming life and Human Potential and Business Executive development, Addiction Coaching, Interventionist, Life Coach—transforming lives and Human Potential Expert, Transpersonal Psychology, Human Motivation, and Mind-Body Psychology. She is also a professional speaker on these areas of specialty and expertise. In addition, Mary is a speaker on breast cancer issues, including how to cope upon diagnosis, treatment, and recovery of cancer.

Dual Masters Degrees
