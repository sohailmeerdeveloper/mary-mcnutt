Aloha And Greetings From Mumbai, New Delhi!
HOME  ALOHA AND GREETINGS FROM MUMBAI, NEW DELHI!
BY GOLDILOCKS

My name is Mary Ellen Mackenna McNutt, M.A., and here I am in Mumbai, India which was such an international experience. Substance abuse is a serious worldwide problem that affects everyone.

I am at this juncture in my life because of God and Hir magnificent grace.  My family was at a point in which we needed and prayed for miracle. We were granted not only one but two. He saved two lives and asked in return that we would save the spiritual lives of others. We were lead to Maui to heal which was also the birthplace of His calling to do the work which He ordained us to do.  It also happened to be my passion.  The study of addiction.  Thus my vision is to build on Maui a world class center for addiction treatment, healing and transition.  A pay it forward mission.  We personally experienced the rich healing spiritual vortex that is Maui and the magnificence of our Creator.
