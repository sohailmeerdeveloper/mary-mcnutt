## Implementation guidance
- Keep the homepage title/H1/copy unchanged for this request.
- Update title, H1, meta description, Open Graph, social title/description, and applicable JSON-LD name consistently.
- Use the route-specific phrase naturally in the opening paragraph and descriptive links. No repetitive meta-keywords tag is required.
- Preserve URLs. Do not make every page compete for the same broad life coach phrase. Focus business coaching on /coaching/ and /services/personal-development-business-growth/; families on the family route; grief on the grief route.
- Legal pages remain legal pages, with coaching as brand context only. The 404 should use Page Not Found | Mary McNutt Coaching and a helpful H1, remain noindex, and be omitted from the sitemap.
- Avoid adding geographic or online availability phrases without confirming those offerings. Preserve accurately sourced credentials; do not adopt competitor credentials, methods, outcomes, or promises.
- Existing service meta descriptions are mechanically truncated, sometimes mid-sentence. The JSON provides complete original replacements.
- Existing family and couples body text conflates coaching with therapy (family: “a form of therapy”; couples: “relationship therapy”). Revise these phrases to coaching/support before presenting the pages as coaching services. Review other clinical service pages for treatment/cure language. No new clinical capability or certification is proposed by these titles.
- Blog article topics remain intact. A personal Mumbai reflection should not be relabeled as a generic how-to coaching guide.
- The 12 Steps/Traditions are educational resources; “coaching” should appear only in surrounding navigation/context if helpful, not jammed into the texts or names.
