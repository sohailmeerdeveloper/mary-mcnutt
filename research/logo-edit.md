# Dragonfly logo update

The client-supplied circular dragonfly replaces the former M monogram in the desktop/mobile header, footer, error page, and favicon.

Edited with the built-in ImageGen tool. Prompt: Preserve the supplied circular mint/lime/aqua swirled background, four wing shapes, pose, proportions, and embossed rendering. Subtly deepen the olive body/lower wings, clarify upper-wing edges and existing veins, and strengthen the immediate soft shadow. No text, new objects, or redesign.

The generated master is archived under `research/assets/mary-dragonfly-contrast-edit.png`. Its exterior checkerboard was excluded through a circular SVG display clip; the logo interior was not redrawn in code. The self-contained website asset is `dist/assets/images/mary-mcnutt-dragonfly-logo.svg`, with an optimized embedded WebP image.
